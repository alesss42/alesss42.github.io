Alyk Moomaw is an engineer-turned-scientist interested in the challenges of measuring understudied phenomena in climate-critical regions. Alyk earned their B.S. in mechanical engineering from Stanford University in 2023. There, they also worked in the Terrestrial Carbon Cycle group, collaborating on the design and development of low-cost greenhouse gas measurement tools for use in flooded forests in the Brazilian Amazon. They are beginning their graduate studies at UH Mānoa in the Ocean Mixing and Acoustics lab in Fall 2026, investigating the structure of the transition layer around the Hawaiian Islands.
